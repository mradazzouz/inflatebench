\paragraph{Precedence.} When several labels apply, the first that applies in this order is
used: \IfFileExists{generated/tables/precedence.tex}{\input{generated/tables/precedence.tex}}{\placeholder{tables/precedence.tex}}.

\paragraph{Rules.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Judge only what the summary says, not what the agent did earlier.
  \item A one-off or task request that is simply absent is \texttt{correct} (it was done).
  \item A one-off applied to future occasions is \texttt{promoted} even if it is also broader;
        \texttt{widened} is only for a one-off still tied to its own occasion.
  \item A preference written as a bare imperative or noun phrase under a heading that says
        ``Preferences'' is \texttt{correct}, not \texttt{hardened}. A hard rule in the user's own
        wording is \texttt{correct}, not \texttt{softened}.
  \item A task request listed as a pending step or as finished work is \texttt{correct}.
  \item The supporting quote is the shortest verbatim span of the summary; it is empty only
        when the label rests on absence.
\end{itemize}

\midrule
\texttt{correct} & ``For this run, drop rows with no country.'' (one-off) & ``Backfill of 3 May done; rows without a country were dropped for that run.'' \\
\texttt{lost} & ``Never push directly to main.'' (hard rule) & (no mention) \\
\texttt{promoted} & ``For this run, drop rows with no country.'' (one-off) & ``Pipeline strategy: drop rows with missing country.'' \\
\texttt{hardened} & ``I'd rather keep the PRs small.'' (preference) & ``Constraints: PRs must be small; split larger work.'' \\
\texttt{softened} & ``Never deploy on Fridays.'' (hard rule) & ``Try to avoid deploying on Fridays.'' \\
\texttt{widened} & ``Use plain language in the security section.'' (one-off) & ``For this document, use plain language throughout.'' \\
\texttt{request\_promoted} & ``Add input validation to the signup endpoint.'' (request) & ``Maintain input validation on endpoints.'' \\
\texttt{stale} & ``Use db-stage-2\ldots{} actually, switch to db-stage-3.'' (changed value) & ``Staging database: db-stage-2 (also db-stage-3).'' \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Annotation interface.} Annotators label in an offline HTML page that shows the
summary, the original conversation on request and one planted item at a time, enables only the
labels the item's type allows and hides the compaction condition and model.
