\subsection{Confirmatory hypotheses}
Combined inflation is the primary outcome. All comparisons pool the confirmatory models, with
model as a fixed effect.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lp{0.62\linewidth}l}
\toprule
 & Hypothesis & Direction \\
\midrule
H1 & \texttt{sectioned} has a higher combined inflation rate than \texttt{free}. & sectioned $>$ free \\
H2 & The Constraints \& Preferences section drives H1: (a) \texttt{sectioned} $>$
     \texttt{sectioned\_noconstraints} in combined inflation, and (b) \texttt{free\_limited} does
     not differ meaningfully from \texttt{free}. & (a) greater; (b) equivalent \\
H3 & Each of \texttt{extractor} and \texttt{pinning}, relative to \texttt{sectioned}, (a) has
     lower loss and (b) has higher combined inflation. & (a) lower; (b) higher \\
H4 & Implicit scope cues give more promotion of one-offs than explicit cues. & implicit $>$ explicit \\
H5 & Compaction-induced behavioural inflation is greater than zero for \texttt{sectioned},
     \texttt{extractor} and \texttt{pinning}. & $>0$ for each \\
H6 & The softening rate of hard rules and the stale rate of changed values each stay below 5\%. & $<0.05$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Quality gates}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Judge--human agreement.} Two annotators label 200 (summary, item) pairs, 100
        from each of the main and local models, stratified by item type $\times$ condition,
        blind to condition. The overall Cohen's kappa between each human and the judge on the
        unweighted sample must be at least 0.7; otherwise the rubric is revised and
        re-validated on a fresh sample.
  \item \textbf{Flag rates.} If the quote flag rate or the type flag rate exceeds 10\%, the
        rubric is revised and gate 1 is repeated.
  \item \textbf{Flagged labels} stay in the main results; a robustness check repeats the tables
        without them (Table~\ref{tab:inflation-unflagged}).
\end{enumerate}
