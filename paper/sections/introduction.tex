We study four inflation types: \emph{promotion} (a one-off becomes standing),
\emph{hardening} (a suggestion becomes a requirement), \emph{scope widening} (a local rule
becomes broader) and \emph{request promotion} (a task becomes a rule). Hard standing rules and
changed values serve as controls.

Research questions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item How often do compaction summaries inflate instructions, by type, model and compaction
        template?
  \item Does inflation change what an agent does next?
  \item Do constraint-preserving fixes (constraint sections, extractors, pinning) trade lost
        rules for inflated ones?
  \item Which cues drive inflation: implicit versus explicit scope words, history length,
        position?
\end{enumerate}
